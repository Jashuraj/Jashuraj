## 🚀 Featured Project: Aadyan (आद्यन्त)
**[Aadyan](https://aadyan.pages.dev/)** is a 100% private, client-side digital career and academic toolkit built specifically for Indian students, job seekers, and government exam aspirants.

- **Zero-Server Compute (₹0 Cost)**: 100% execution in browser RAM via Canvas API, Web Audio, and PDF-lib.
- **DPDP Act 2023 Compliant**: Zero student documents, marksheets, signatures, or photos ever touch a remote server.
- **Core Utilities**:
  - 📸 **Exam Readiness Bundle & Photo Resizer**: 1-click batch bundling for SSC CGL, UPSC, IBPS, and State PSCs.
  - 🖨️ **4×6" Passport Photo Print Sheet**: 300 DPI bulk print grid with cutting guides for local xerox printing at ₹5.
  - ⌨️ **SSC DEST Typing Speed Test**: Real-time Gross/Net WPM and KDPH calculation with printable proficiency certificates.
  - 📄 **1-Page ATS Resume Builder**: Clean-room ATS resume generator with JD keyword matcher and placement slips.
  - 🎓 **Comprehensive Indian CGPA Converter**: Statutory conversion formulas for 150+ Indian universities and boards.
  - 💰 **In-Hand Salary & Education Loan Calculators**: Indian statutory calculations for Section 10(13A), Section 80E, and EPF/ESIC.
